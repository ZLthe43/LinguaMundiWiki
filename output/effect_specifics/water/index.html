"> Water Magic

Water is a complex element, focussing on the conjuration and careful, precise control of matter. Indeed, water is a malleable substance with many properties. Practitioners often find water to be the element best suited for creating complex spells, patterns and constructs, but of course, this requires a hefty amount of computation.

Water Needle

vec, vec, num →
Pops a number for power, followed by a vector for direction and a vector for origin. Shoots a projectile at power-value blocks per tick. Deals damage equal to the product of 1.75 times speed, with a cap of 40 damage.

Mana cost: 5 + pow^1.6

Water Slash

vec, vec, num →
Pops a number for power, followed by a vector for direction and a vector for origin. Shoots a wide, short-lived water construct that slices through any target in its path, dealing damage equal to its power, capped at 20.

Mana cost: 10 + 1.4 * pow^1.6

Gills

entity, num →
Pops a number representing power and a target which must be a LivingEntity. Applies Water Breathing to the target entity, with each power level adding 10 seconds of the effect to the target.

Mana cost: 30 * pow

Fins

entity, num →
Pops a number representing power and a target which must be a LivingEntity. Applies Dolphin’s Grace to the target entity, with each power level adding 10 seconds of the effect to the target.

Mana cost: 20 * pow

Create Water

position →
Pops a block position at the head of the capsum and conjures a water source at the given position.

Mana cost: 10

Summon Rain

→
Summons rain the instant it is cast.

Mana cost: 80

Clear Weather

→
Clears rain the instant it is cast.

Mana cost: 80

Extinguish

vec, num →
Pops a number representing power, and a vector representing position. Extinguishes all fire, from open flames to campfires, and heals all entities within a range through the restorative essence of water.

$(li)Radius = 2 + 0.3 * pow, capped at 5 blocks.$(li)Healing = pow, capped at 15 health.

Mana cost: 15 + 2.5 * pow^1.7

Water Ball

vec, vec, num, quote → result
Pops a number describing power, followed by a vector describing direction and a vector describing origin. Shoots a projectile that deals damage equivalent to the given power value, with a cap of 20 damage.

The water ball also pushes an entity species representing itself, allowing casters to fully control its trajectory, and use the species to anchor other effects.

The projectile calls the given quote upon impact, ending the spell once this quote is run, regardless of any instructions remaining on the cursorium.

Mana cost: 20 + 1.5 * pow^1.5
